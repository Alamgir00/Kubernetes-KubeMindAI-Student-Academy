Recommended learning loop

  1. Read the lesson objectives and technical notes.
  2. Explore the topic-specific architecture by clicking components.
  3. Play the operational flow and use Previous, Next, speed and Restart.
  4. Work through the YAML, commands and practical lab in the lesson.
  5. Open the simulator to practise commands and failure scenarios.
  6. Mark the day complete and take the 20-question assessment when ready.
